## 1. The pricing model — end to end (headline)

Billing was rebuilt three times as the model sharpened, ending at à-la-carte + per-seat:

- **Per-domain SaaS modules** — each feature (Websites, CRM, Brain, Email, …) became a sellable SKU with bundle pricing, `billingMode` (agency/saas/byok), and usage alerts.
- **3-tier plans** (Starter/Growth/Scale) layered on top — first-class Stripe products, purchasable checkout, BYOK gated to Scale ("the BYOK inversion": marked-up metered AI is the profit center; BYOK is the Scale unlock).
- **Dropped the tiers** → **pure à-la-carte modules + volume discounts** (4/8/12 modules → 10/20/30% off, baked into computed Stripe `price_data` line items, not coupons). BYOK became "contact sales."
- **Per-seat pricing** on top: `total = M + (seats−1) × min(M, $30)`, M = post-discount module subtotal. Seats bill on accept; the **`recomputeClientSubscription` reconciler** became the single writer of every subscription.
- **Lifecycle & plumbing:** completed the Stripe subscription webhook lifecycle, monthly AI-credit grants on renewal, Stripe Customer Portal + payment-method detach, live product/price IDs wired into the catalog, fail-closed entitlement gating with API/MCP service guards.
